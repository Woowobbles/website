Leading product design at an accessible tech startup

The Present Pal app was originally built without design expertise and so had a variety of usability, accessibility, and design issues. There was also substantial tech-debt, so we decided that the best approach would be for a full redesign and rebuild.

Objectives:

Redesign the app to be more accessible and easier to use. Re-engage dormant users and increase future usage of the app.

Accessibility focused design

We worked with a former Senior Accessibility Engineer at Apple, and recruited a neurodiverse group of users for regular usability testing to ensure that our designs met the accessibility needs of our users.

Outcomes

The new Present Pal was easier for users to use, more accessible, and allowed us to expand our market within the UK, and into educational institutions in the United States.

Since releasing the new Present Pal:

  • The company has tripled its market share in the industry.
  • We managed to re-engage and convert dozens of dormant customers.
  • The product's Sean Ellis Test results reached 52%.
