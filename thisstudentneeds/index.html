Redesigning a support resource for university students with disabilities

This Student Needs had created a website to help disabled students navigate the process of applying for university support. As the service grew, the team wanted to better understand how students were using the guide and identify opportunities to make it more accessible and effective.

Objectives:

Understand the needs and challenges of students using the guide, identify barriers within the existing experience, and redesign the website to make information easier to find, understand, and act on.

Understanding the needs of disabled students

I interviewed students with disabilities to uncover opportunities to make content clearer, navigation more intuitive, and the experience more accessible.

Outcomes

The project gave the This Student Needs team valuable insight into their users while delivering a redesigned website that was clearer, more engaging, and easier to navigate. Following launch, the team reported increased sign-ups and returning users, alongside positive feedback on the improved experience
